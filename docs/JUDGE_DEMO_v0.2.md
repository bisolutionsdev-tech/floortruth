# 3-minute FloorTruth demo (v0.2)

### 0:00–0:25 — Why

"A typical enterprise already has surveys, HR tools and dashboards. What it often lacks is an auditable loop from a frontline observation to an independently tested improvement. FloorTruth makes that loop visible."

### 0:25–0:55 — Capture and evidence

Show nine **synthetic** observations. Open *Equipment readiness across shifts*. Click through four linked observations across shifts. Emphasize correlation does *not* establish root cause.

### 0:55–1:35 — Reason and challenge

If activated: call Muse Spark; display two alternative root-cause hypotheses with original source IDs, falsifiers and missing evidence. Explain model results are schema-constrained then checked locally against actual observation IDs. Without credits: show deterministic fallback and admit it is a partial demo.

### 1:35–2:20 — Govern and recover

Approve a simulated pilot. Show evidence fingerprint and audit timeline. Add a new shift-handoff observation; explain the previously approved snapshot becomes **Needs re-review**. Re-approve, then measure the synthetic outcome. Show rollback event. The model cannot approve operational changes.

### 2:20–2:50 — Measure honestly

Display baseline 34 min/shift, observed 22, 40 shifts, $35/hour labor-equivalent value and $120 intervention cost → **synthetic $160 descriptive net estimate**. Explicitly note this is not proof of causality or realized savings.

### 2:50–3:00 — Close

"FloorTruth converts fragmented employee observations into traceable cases, governed tests, and cumulative organizational learning. AI proposes; humans decide; observed outcomes determine what survives."

Avoid saying "production-ready", "proven enterprise savings", "tamper-proof ledger", "fully implemented role-based access", or "real employee data".
